About

We build the things we advise on

Solution Anything is a software consultancy and product studio. We work with organisations of all sizes, from startups finding their feet on Salesforce to enterprises untangling years of platform debt. Our advice comes from doing the work, not just reviewing it.

We also build and maintain our own products. The Any+ Suite, our free AppExchange package, grew out of problems we kept solving for clients. If we build it once and it's useful, we ship it for everyone. That practitioner mindset shapes everything we do.

We use the latest AI-assisted development tools across every engagement. That means faster delivery, fewer iterations and more competitive pricing without cutting corners on quality.

Products

Our products

The Any+ Suite

Four Lightning Web Components in one package. Drop them on any record page, pick an object and configure in clicks. No code, no data leaving your org.

Explore the Any+ Suite

AnyCal+

Show related records as calendar events. Monthly, weekly and daily views with drag-and-drop rescheduling.

AnyKanban+

Drag-and-drop Kanban board driven by any picklist field. Cards move between columns and update the record instantly.

AnyGantt+

Scrollable Gantt timeline. Pick start and end date fields and records appear as colour-coded bars across day, week or month views.

AnyHierarchy+

Expandable tree view for self-referencing objects. Pick a parent lookup field and see the full ancestor and descendant chain.
